# Artwork provenance

The current cover, social preview and graphics-failure fallback are actual game
renders using the NASA rover model and credited Poly Haven surfaces. See
[the asset ledger](../public/ASSETS.csv) and [bibliography](BIBLIOGRAPHY.md).

## Removed prototype artwork

The AI-generated `public/images/mars-landscape.png` prototype was removed from
the source tree and distribution in v0.19.8 (November 15, 2026). The graphics
fallback now uses `public/images/last-light-hero.webp`, captured from the game.
This historical record does not describe a currently shipped asset. Removing
it does not change the project's AI-assisted development history.

## Historical generation prompt (removed asset)


Use case: stylized-concept. Create a visually breathtaking cinematic environment image for an emotionally moving browser game about NASA's Opportunity rover. Landscape 16:9, high resolution. Vast rolling Martian desert at very late golden dusk, dusty rust-red and muted burnt-umber dunes, craggy layered rocky horizon, faint pale sun low in dusty peach haze. A scientifically recognizable small six-wheeled Opportunity Mars rover with rectangular blue-black solar-panel deck and twin camera mast stands alone in the right third, lower half, seen from behind at three-quarter view facing faraway horizon. Rover should occupy roughly 18 percent of image width, detailed metal and dusty wheels, long delicate tire tracks lead from lower foreground toward it. Tender lonely scale, absolutely gorgeous film composition, cinematic painterly realism with natural grain and soft atmospheric perspective, subtle sunset light on the rover panels, no cartoon eyes, no anthropomorphic face. Upper left and left middle spacious soft dark neutral umber atmosphere for overlay typography, foreground dark rocky sand. Mars sky only, no visible Earth planet, no buildings or astronauts. No text, logos, labels, frames, borders, UI, or watermark. This is original dramatic artwork, not a NASA photograph. Palette subdued burnt orange, sepia, ink brown and warm cream with dark blue panels; avoid neon or oversaturated red. Make evocative haunting beauty and wide emptiness the focus.
