# ROOM-48 — control-room art direction

Exactly one GPT-6 Astra subagent authored the visual pass; no other agents were
spawned for it. Root integrated and reviewed desktop and portrait renders.
The design moves from generic dark furniture to a cared-for after-hours mission
workstation: layered architecture, tactile equipment, a motivated articulated
lamp, notebook, ceramic mug, cables and a physically curled handwritten Oppy-heart
note. The note is authored fiction, not a recovered artifact or NASA document.

The physical monitor and its Earth-to-Mars instrument retain their transforms and
behavior. No additional WebGL context, dependency, external texture or downloaded
logo was added. All new marks/material textures are deterministic procedural art.
NASA/JPL identification is ordinary setting text, not an invented agency logo.
NASA's identity guidance was checked at:
https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/brand-guidelines/

Initial critical review found the wall plaque and small surface lettering hidden
inside bevels, as well as insufficient back-wall light. Those were corrected before
final acceptance. The room remains a stylized interpretation, not a reconstruction
of a particular NASA/JPL facility. Portrait prioritizes the monitor/note; peripheral
desk items naturally leave the narrower view.
