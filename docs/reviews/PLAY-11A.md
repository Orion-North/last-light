## Verdict

It finishes and communicates its historical ending clearly, but the player mostly operates a slideshow in front of an attractive rover. The repeated receipt establishes a useful ritual. The current action layer does too little to earn the memories that ritual is supposed to make precious. Removing Autodrive alone will add steering time, not interesting decisions.

## Prioritized changes

1. **P1 — Make discovery an observable action.** Last Chance opens on a huge, visibly layered rock, yet progress stays at zero and instructs “Turn toward the layered face.” Berry Bowl says “Move closer” while the only control hint is “Right stick: look”; the rover deck obscures much of the grain target. The visible science and hidden acceptance test disagree. Both assists immediately produce the answer/choices. Put a visible aim cue on the feature, show why a view qualifies, frame the camera without rover obstruction, and make assistance demonstrate the same operation. Better: align a tool with a layer, trace its crossing, or brush a patch and visibly expose its surface. The player must cause the evidence to appear before receiving its interpretation.

2. **P1 — Replace repeated text selection with different world verbs.** Witnessed route after geology: Look back along tracks → timed scan → prose pair; Take in valley → timed scan → prose pair; Look at Sun → same; Revisit memories → same; final look → same. The verbs promise looking, comparison, and recollection but button presses do the work. Let the player turn the mast to compose a horizon, follow actual tracks, compare exposed grains, locate their shadow, and aim the antenna. Use a compact acknowledgment after each completed action. Keep a small number of explicit choices where the distinction actually matters.

3. **P1 — Give transmission a readable journey and changed failure behavior.** Squeeze/release works and its readiness/receipt are legible. The destination is a tiny text label above a large abstract bracket; the landscape carries little causal action. Animate the antenna initiating a packet, a clearly visible route toward Earth, an arrival, then a returning acknowledgment. On the last image visibly lose part of that packet/image; on recovery calls visibly send out and receive nothing. Fragile-link scenes otherwise felt operationally identical to clear ones. Preserve text equivalents and fixed historical outcome.

4. **P2 — Make manual travel purposeful.** Baseline Autodrive produces passive 20–30 second legs toward a beacon, with the action HUD hidden. Six destinations occupy the same small arena. The 2015 marathon is an approximately 25-second local trip beside familiar rocks. Replace assistance with a short readable route and tangible local choices: approach side, safe gap, viewpoint, optional curiosity. Provide direction and distance continuously, and immediate arrival feedback. Exploration currently promises freedom but provides no witnessed incidental discoveries/rewards.

5. **P2 — Let memories preserve visible work.** I chose horizon, crossing layers, brushed patch, tracks, mast shadow, people's care, water discoveries, little light, recovery commands, science archive, and “You have done enough.” The ending correctly retains all eleven, but primarily as prose titles/descriptions; it cannot replay an image I composed or a patch I actually brushed. Show the selected view/feature beside its send and again at the ending. Recollection can then depend on something I did, not the adjective I selected.

6. **P2 — Give Earth a distinct playable instrument.** The handover explicitly names my new role and pulls away from the rover; that is clear. Yet Earth-side actions still sit over Mars and reuse choice → charge → reply. Put the recovery channel/receiver in the foreground, let the player issue one concrete call and hold/listen to the empty response, then carry the rover view as the remote destination. Do not make three prose selections carry the entire human recovery effort.
