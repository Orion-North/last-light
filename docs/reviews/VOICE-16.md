# VOICE-16 — Attention before elegy

Source review only, not a playthrough. Read the required project overviews, `terminal-story.js`, `story.js`, `time.js`, `briefing.js`, `story-console.js`, relevant `main.js` integration, and the repository's source register. No playtesting or fresh external factual verification. No code changes.

Opportunity should sound like a curious working presence: particular about what is under its camera, pleased when a comparison yields something, accustomed to receiving an answer. Attachment should accumulate through that habit. Currently it often sounds like a retrospective narrator explaining why the player should care.

## What weakens the voice

- “My world was never only what I could see from here” and “I have made this small, ordinary afternoon something we share” conclude the emotional argument for us. They could accompany almost any action.
- “while it is still here” makes the ordinary afternoon knowingly precious. “Whatever happens to this sky” and “They have so much more of the journey than this” begin the farewell early. Opportunity should encounter a difficult day, not know its narrative function.
- “They will be there for the next one” asserts knowledge of the future. Habit is playable; certainty is unnecessary.
- “might tell me where it went” makes Last Chance sound like a search for where water disappeared, rather than evidence of ancient flow. “They see the water” also needs care: the player sees layers, not water itself.
- The repeated “I can show them” / “another clue” / “already home” cadence makes curiosity feel administered. Long arrival thoughts repeat identity and mission already introduced in the briefing.

## Eight candidate lines, each earned by an event

These are alternatives for selected beats, not eight extra captions. All are invented inner voice, never telemetry or historical quotations.

1. **First ground selection, before send:** “Ground beneath my wheels. I want them to see that first.” Keep the existing horizon alternative equally concrete.
2. **Berry Bowl arrival, once stationary:** “They call them blueberries. I have never seen the ones on Earth. I wonder what made them think of these.” One longer, affectionate oddity gives the rover character without interrupting driving.
3. **Last Chance successful receipt:** “Those crossing layers are evidence that water once moved here, where I am standing.” Tool completion retains the observed crossed paths; this receipt alone adds the sourced interpretation.
4. **Berry Bowl successful receipt:** “Hematite in the grains. Another clue that water helped form them.” This imagined interpretation draws on mission sources. At Compare, retain the two relative iron-bearing readings; they do not directly identify hematite.
5. **Marathon survey complete:** “Forty-two kilometres. I can only see the last few tracks.” Use only if those tracks are actually visible in the survey; otherwise: “Forty-two kilometres. There is more ground ahead.”
6. **Ordinary valley observation complete:** “That bright edge of stone. I’ll send that.” No warning that its light will end.
7. **Storm sky observation complete:** “I know where the Sun should be. I can barely make it out.” Verify the actual rendered sky supports this; otherwise use “There is less light through the dust.”
8. **Final observed frame, before departure:** “I’ll send this view.” Then let the incomplete delivery be discovered through feedback, without an invented farewell.

## Writers' room revision

STORY-16 and DELIVERY-16 converge on physical results at tool completion, then one qualified water interpretation at successful receipt. The crossed paths and paired readings explain what is worth sending; the journal supports recovery, never primary comprehension. Do not repeat the interpretation at completion and receipt. Root challenges uniformly tiny mechanical lines; the berry-name thought supplies roomier curiosity. Keep the actual first-choice recollection at the later revisit, separate from final transmission. Reject “I have the Sun’s direction. I’ll try there”: it suggests a solution and an unverified precise action. Remove the generic transmitting thought and most repeated receipt morals. Keep controls responsive: no mandatory reading dwell. The final thought ends before contact loss; Earth recovery receives no rover voice.
