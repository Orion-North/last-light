# STORY-16 — make the work carry the story

## Diagnosis

Only two scenes now require menus, but the dramatic grammar still resembles choosing and sending text. Arrival anticipates the discovery, completed work explains it, a generic sending thought interrupts, and receipt explains it again. The player performs useful physical actions; prose repeatedly supplies their meaning before the relationship can develop through feedback.

The geology also blurs observation, measurement and interpretation. Crossing lines do not tell us “where [water] went.” Berry Bowl needs the comparison between cleaned rock and grains to remain legible; a camera view alone cannot establish hematite. Sending should connect the player's work to collective understanding without implying an instantaneous historical scientific announcement.

The voice often knows its own ending. “While it is still here,” “whatever happens,” and “so much more of the journey than this” prepare a eulogy instead of allowing an ordinary task to be interrupted. Repeated assurances that discoveries are already home explain the consolation before the loss occurs.

## Dramatic spine

**Arrive and investigate:** establish identity and purpose once, then let attention do the characterization. Opening thought: “I am Opportunity. I came to look for signs of ancient water.” First playable cue remains “Look through your cameras,” including after skipping the introduction. The first successful return gets “They have it.” The signal's return, visible without sound, establishes the relationship.

**Learn by doing:** arrival asks “Why do these layers cross?” Tools remain quiet while hands and eyes work. Completion notices what the player actually uncovered. Berry Bowl example: “The grains read differently from the brushed rock.” Following transmission, one qualified interpretation: “Their comparison points to hematite in the grains—another clue to ancient water.” The receipt is operational; this remains Opportunity's imagined thought, never purported Earth dialogue. Last Chance similarly gets one water interpretation after inspection and successful sharing. Detailed attribution stays in the journal.

**Make time ordinary:** one thought across the first passage: “Eleven years. Still another place to look.” Dates establish chronology. Further light cycles need no narration. The ordinary afternoon says “A bright edge on that stone.” Its successful return can be wordless. Do not announce that this light is precious.

**Interrupt the routine:** dust visibly removes the horizon; one factual connection is enough: “Less sunlight reaches my panels.” Preserve the learned send/return interval. Final prepared thought: “I'll send this view.” No new thought during transmission. The incomplete frame and absent return carry the rupture; no invented final words or farewell.

**Move to the other end:** rover thought stays absent permanently. Persistent factual transition, outside the inner voice: “EARTH · AFTER JUNE 10, 2018. Contact with Opportunity has been lost.” Action: “Send a recovery command from Earth.” It survives skipping the passage. Reverse the established signal direction. Distinguish contact loss, eight months of recovery, February 12 final commands, and February 13 mission closure.

**Finish with evidence:** after the final unanswered call, neutral factual text: “February 13, 2019. NASA declares the mission complete.” Show records earned through the player's investigations; no compulsory summary of what grief means.
